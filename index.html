03 / Meet the artist

Hello, I'm Vita.

I make tattoos that feel like they could only belong to you.

Originally from Ukraine, I work in Teddington, London with a love for fine line, graphic detail and personal ideas. I think about the way a design moves with your body as much as the way it looks on paper.

The process is calm and collaborative: we talk, sketch, refine, and make something worth carrying.

Vita Sa.li Tattoo

04 / The process

From the first idea to the final line.

No rushed choices. Just a thoughtful design, shaped together from the first conversation to the moment it becomes yours.

  1. 01

    Tell me your idea

    Bring a memory, reference, feeling or rough thought. We start with a conversation.

  2. 02

    We shape the design

    I sketch for your placement, proportions and the details that matter to you.

  3. 03

    Make it permanent

    We refine the final piece together, then take our time to bring it to life.
